# TagYourBeat: The AI Studio That Makes Your Beat Say Your Name
 
If you've spent any time on BeatStars or YouTube type-beat channels, you already know the sound: two seconds of a voice — "so and so on the beat" — stamped over the intro of every track. That's a producer tag, and it's how listeners learn to recognize a producer before they ever see a name. **[TagYourBeat](https://tagyourbeat.com/)** is a free-to-try AI studio built around exactly that: type a line, pick a voice, and get a DAW-ready producer tag in about 30 seconds — no microphone, no session booking, no voice-over gig queue.
 
This article is a full tour of the site: the core tag studio, the family of tag and DJ drop tools, five name generators, and a growing library of beat licensing guides.
 
## First, What Is a Producer Tag?
 
![Flat illustration of a studio microphone projecting a sound wave that loops across the scene and ends as a signature-like ribbon over a beat pad and a vinyl record](https://artworks.tagyourbeat.com/imgs/generated/what-is-a-producer-tag.webp)
 
A producer tag (also called a beat tag or voice tag) is the vocal signature a producer places on their instrumentals. It works three jobs at once: branding (your sound becomes recognizable), protection (tagged beats are harder to re-upload and resell), and credit (the tag travels with the beat wherever it's shared). If you're new to the concept, the site's beginner guide walks through the anatomy of a tag, where to place it, and how the famous ones were made: [What Is a Producer Tag?](https://tagyourbeat.com/what-is-a-producer-tag)
 
For inspiration, browse [100+ producer tag ideas](https://tagyourbeat.com/producer-tag-ideas) or see how the classics — from Metro Boomin to Murda Beatz — built theirs in [famous producer tags, dissected](https://tagyourbeat.com/famous-producer-tags).
 
## The Core: The Producer Tag Generator Studio
 
![Flat illustration of a home studio desk with a microphone, a laptop showing a waveform, and a sparkle icon for AI generation](https://artworks.tagyourbeat.com/imgs/generated/voice-tag-generator.webp)
 
The [producer tag generator](https://tagyourbeat.com/) on the homepage is the heart of the site. The workflow is deliberately short:
 
1. **Type your line** — your producer name, a catchphrase, whatever your beats should say.
2. **Pick one of 8 voices** — male and female reads tuned for trap, boom bap, drill, melodic, and more. You can audition each voice before committing.
3. **Shape it with a Style Kit** — 10 processing presets that transform a plain read into a signature sound: *Stutter* chops the first word and drops an 808 under it, *Deep* drags the voice under a sub boom for dark trap, *Radio* spins your name out of dusty vinyl crackle, *Slowed* gives that chopped-and-screwed haze, and so on. Each kit has an A/B demo so you hear the before and after.
4. **Export** — paid generations deliver studio-grade files: 24-bit WAV and MP3, a Dry + Wet stems ZIP for mixing the tag into your own chain, plus a commercial rights certificate.
 
You get **2 free generations with no signup** — just open the site and try it. The full [how to make a producer tag](https://tagyourbeat.com/how-to-make-a-producer-tag) guide compares the three ways to get one (AI studio, recording it yourself, or hiring a voice artist) with honest cost and effort breakdowns.

## DJ Drops: Your Name Over Every Set
 
![Flat illustration of a DJ behind a mixer and turntables raising one hand, a microphone sending a bold sound wave across a crowd silhouette with raised hands](https://artworks.tagyourbeat.com/imgs/generated/dj-name-drops.webp)
 
A DJ drop is a producer tag's louder sibling — the "you're listening to DJ ___" staple of mixes, radio sets, and club nights. TagYourBeat devotes a whole cluster to it:
 
- The [DJ drops generator](https://tagyourbeat.com/dj-drops) — type a line, pick a voice, shape it with club-ready Style Kits like Radio or Hype, and export a WAV without booking studio time.
- [DJ name drops](https://tagyourbeat.com/dj-name-drops) — focused on the name-drop format specifically.
- [Custom DJ drops](https://tagyourbeat.com/custom-dj-drops) — briefed like a pro session: name, pronunciation, pack, rights — then cut in about 30 seconds, or take the same brief to any voice artist.
 
For the words themselves, [DJ drop scripts](https://tagyourbeat.com/dj-drop-scripts) collects battle-tested script patterns, and two guides cover the fundamentals: [what is a DJ drop](https://tagyourbeat.com/what-is-a-dj-drop) and [how to make a DJ drop](https://tagyourbeat.com/how-to-make-a-dj-drop). Everything lives on the [DJ drop tools shelf](https://tagyourbeat.com/dj-drop-tools).
